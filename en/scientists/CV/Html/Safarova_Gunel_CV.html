Profile

Gunel Safarova is a change-management consultant at Onepoint in France, where she has worked since 2022, and an independent consultant in France and Azerbaijan since 2018. She received a doctorate in management sciences in 2019. The thesis, supervised by Prof. Annie Bartoli, is on the management of innovation and ambidexterity in the American context, in the case of family firms. She holds two Master 2 degrees, in business administration from the University of Versailles Saint-Quentin-en-Yvelines (2012) and in trilingual international management from Paris-Est Créteil University (2010). She teaches at ADA University in Azerbaijan and at the University of Versailles.
