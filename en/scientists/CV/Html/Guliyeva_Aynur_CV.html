Profile

Aynur Guliyeva is an associate professor at the University of Technology Tarbes Occitanie Pyrénées, where she has worked since January 2024. She received a PhD in chemistry of materials from the University of Orléans in 2016–2019, a Master 2 in polymers for advanced technologies from the University of Grenoble Alpes in 2015–2016, and a bachelor’s degree in chemistry from Baku State University in 2010–2014. She was a scientist at Lonza Capsugel in Colmar, a research engineer at PIMM, ENSAM in Paris, and a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Delaware. Published 8 articles.
