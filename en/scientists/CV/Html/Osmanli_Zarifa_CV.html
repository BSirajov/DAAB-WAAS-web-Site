Profile

Zarifa Osmanli is a postdoctoral fellow at BioComputing UP at the University of Padua, where she has worked since January 2024 on protein structure–function relations in cancer. She received a PhD in structural bioinformatics from the University of Montpellier and CRBM-CNRS in November 2019–December 2023, on bioinformatics tools for structural annotation of alternative proteomes. She holds an MSc in molecular biology from Ege University (2014–2017) and a BSc in biophysics from Baku State University (2009–2013). She is also a junior research assistant at the Institute of Biophysics in Baku (2019–present).
