Profile

Valeh Rustamov is a postdoctoral researcher and group leader at the Institute of Molecular and Cell Biology of Mannheim University of Applied Sciences, under Prof. Dr. Hafner and Prof. Dr. Rudolf, since 2020. He completed a doctorate in 2014–2019 at the Medical Faculty Mannheim of Heidelberg University, with distinction, on a therapeutic antibody against bone sialoprotein in 3D cell culture. He qualified as a dentist at the Azerbaijan Medical University (2008–2013) and as a dental technician at Baku Basic Medical College No. 2 (2006–2008). Two journal articles are listed, both with him as an author, in Frontiers in Oncology (2019) and the Journal of Cellular Biotechnology (2017).
