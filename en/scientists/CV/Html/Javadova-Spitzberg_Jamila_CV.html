Profile

Jamila Javadova-Spitzberg is an assistant professor at the University of Texas at Arlington, where she teaches organ and harpsichord. She holds a Doctor of Musical Arts degree in performance from the University of North Texas (2008), a Master of Music from the Azerbaijan State Conservatory (1986), and further qualifications from the Conservatorium van Amsterdam, the Associated Board of the Royal Schools of Music, and the Leopold Mozart Center in Augsburg. She performs on organ, harpsichord, and historical fortepiano, and in 2017 she founded the Azerbaijan-American Music Foundation, of which she is executive director. In 2022 she received the Tarraggi Medal of the Republic of Azerbaijan.
