Profile

Akif Suleymanov is professor at the Higher School of Law of Turan University, where he has worked since 1 September 2022. He defended a Doctor of Legal Sciences dissertation in 2010 at the Academy of the Ministry of Internal Affairs of the Republic of Kazakhstan. The thesis is on the theoretical and applied problems of public oversight of executive authorities. The scientific adviser was E. B. Mukhamedzhanov. He also defended a candidate dissertation on the constitutional and legal foundations of interaction between the President and the Constitutional Council, supervised by M. T.-M. Baymakhanov. He received a higher legal education in jurisprudence at Al-Farabi Kazakh National University in 1994–1998 and a master’s degree in state law and administration there in 1998–2000. The Higher Attestation Commission awarded him the associate professor title in 2008.
