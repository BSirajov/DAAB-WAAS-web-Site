Profile

Khagani Gayibli is director of the Turkish Language Center at the University of Tartu, a post dated from 1998. He received a PhD in history from the University of Tartu in 2008–2011 and an MA in Uralic languages there in 2003–2005. He has been chair of the Estonia-Azerbaijan Society since 1994 and a member and deputy chair of the board of the Congress of World Azerbaijanis since 2001. Published books of essays, poetry, and Turkish-Estonian language guides, and translations from Azerbaijani, Estonian, and Turkish. He was awarded the Taraggi Medal on 21 April 2022.
