Profile

Ilham Akhundov is director of the undergraduate mathematics, business, and accounting programs in the Faculty of Mathematics at the University of Waterloo, a post he has held since 2013. He received a PhD in mathematics, specializing in probability theory and mathematical statistics, from St. Petersburg University in 1988, and a bachelor’s degree in mathematics there in 1981. He was deputy director academic of the University of Waterloo campus in the United Arab Emirates in 2009–2013, a lecturer at Waterloo in 2007–2009, and an assistant professor at McMaster University in 2000–2007. Earlier he was an associate professor at Odlar Yurdu University and at Azerbaijan State Economic University.
