Profile

Ismihan Bayramoglu is a professor at Izmir University of Economics, where he has worked since 2001. He was dean of the Faculty of Arts and Sciences from 2001 to 2022 and chair of the Department of Mathematics from 2001 to 2015. He received a PhD from Kiev State University in 1988. The dissertation is on invariant confidence intervals and statistical criteria based on training samples. Holds a master’s degree and a BSc in applied mathematics from Azerbaijan State University (1981). From 1993 to 2001 he was a professor in the Department of Statistics at Ankara University. Publications also use the spelling Bairamov.
