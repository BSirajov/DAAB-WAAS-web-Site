Profile

Yashar Musayev is head of development for new energy business at Siemens Energy AG in Erlangen and Munich, an executive post he has held since May 2020. The work covers green hydrogen, electrolysis, fuel technology, and renewable energy, with about 32 direct reports. He holds a Dr.-Ing. from Friedrich-Alexander University Erlangen-Nuremberg, from doctoral research in 1998–2001, and a Dipl.-Ing. in mechanical engineering technology from the Technical University of Azerbaijan (1984–1991), with distinction. From 2005 to 2020 he worked at Schaeffler AG, including as senior vice president and head of the Competency Center for Surface Technology from 2015 to 2020.
