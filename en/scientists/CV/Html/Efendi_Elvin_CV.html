Profile

Elvin Afandi is division head for corporate strategy and research at the Islamic Corporation for the Development of the Private Sector of the Islamic Development Bank Group in Jeddah, a post he has held since January 2022. He received a PhD in economics from Baku State University in 2006–2008, with the thesis Essays on Pro-poor Growth, an MSc in economics from Azerbaijan State University of Economics in 2003–2005, and a BSc in economics there in 1999–2003. He completed postgraduate study in development economics at Michigan State University in 2009–2010. He has taught at Dar Al-Hekma University, Azerbaijan State University of Economics, and Khazar University.
