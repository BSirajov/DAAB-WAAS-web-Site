Profile

Gafar Mehdiyev is a professor at Erciyes University in Kayseri, in the Department of Armenian Language and Culture, a title he has held since 2016. He has been a faculty member there since 2010. He graduated from the Faculty of Journalism of Baku State University in 1980 and received a PhD in political sciences in 2007 for a dissertation on the role of the press in bringing out facts about the Armenian question, based on Armenian and Turkish press materials. He worked as a journalist and editor in Baku from 1980 to 2017 and taught at Baku State University from 2004 to 2012. Published more than 100 articles and about 20 books.
