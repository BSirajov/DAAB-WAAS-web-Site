Profile

Ebulfez Suleymanov was head of the sociology department at Üsküdar University in Istanbul from January 2012 until August 2024. From November 2022 until August 2024 he was also at the International Turkic Academy in Astana. Title: Prof. Dr. and does not name a doctorate. Published more than 150 scientific works, including 5 monographs (12 articles in Web of Science or Scopus) and more than 100 conference papers. Google Scholar: h-index 10; i10-index 11.
