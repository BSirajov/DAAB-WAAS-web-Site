Consistency conventions used in this review
  • Use American English, including commas and periods inside closing quotation marks. Retain official names and titles pending verification.
  • Use Ph.D. consistently in prose, matching the page’s rendered credential labels. Retain foreign degrees and ranks; do not assume that Candidate of Sciences, Doctor of Sciences, docent, and American degrees or ranks are equivalent.
  • Use sentence case for descriptive roles in prose and a consistent heading style. Lowercase ordinary disciplines unless part of an official title. Affiliation fragments need no final period.
  • Use month–day–year dates and en dashes in numerical ranges. Retain all dates and quantities, including approximate counts.
  • Use disciplines under “Field” and separate multiple affiliations clearly. Confirm official institution names, personal spellings, and transliterations before standardizing them.
