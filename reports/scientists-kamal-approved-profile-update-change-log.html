Kamal Akbarov, PhD, DMSc, MBA

Radiation oncologist and healthcare project management specialist

Kamal Akbarov is a radiation oncologist with extensive clinical, research, and international experience in cancer treatment, healthcare project management, and professional education. His work focuses on improving radiotherapy services, strengthening cancer control programs, and developing international scientific cooperation.

He holds a PhD and a Doctor of Medical Sciences degree in Radiation Oncology from Azerbaijan’s National Center of Oncology, as well as an MBA in International Healthcare Management.

In 2024, he joined the World Health Organization (WHO) as a Technical Officer for chemical, biological, radiological, and nuclear (CBRN) threats. His responsibilities include providing scientific and technical support for activities in Ukraine under the WHO Health Emergencies Program.

From 2018 to 2023, he worked as a Technical Officer and Radiation Oncologist at the International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA) in Vienna. He supported technical cooperation projects, the development of national cancer control programs, radiotherapy planning, equipment procurement, and professional training. In 2021, he supported 61 technical cooperation projects with a combined budget of $331 million.

He led scientific and practical aspects of the United Nations Global Joint Program on Cervical Cancer and the ELAISA research project on e-learning interventions for AI-assisted contouring skills in radiotherapy. He contributed to the organization of ICARO3, ESTRO teaching courses, GEC-ESTRO workshops, and regional projects.

From 2005 to 2018, he worked as a Radiation Oncologist and Scientific Officer at Azerbaijan’s National Center of Oncology in Baku. His clinical experience includes 3D conformal radiotherapy, intensity-modulated radiotherapy (IMRT), volumetric modulated arc therapy (VMAT), stereotactic body radiotherapy (SBRT), stereotactic radiosurgery (SRS), image-guided and interstitial brachytherapy, and concurrent chemotherapy. He also contributed to multidisciplinary treatment planning and the introduction of modern radiotherapy techniques.

His research includes cervical cancer treatment, personalized radiosensitization, adaptive brachytherapy, and concurrent chemoradiotherapy. He participated in the EMBRACE II multicenter research project and has worked on radiotherapy contouring education, AI-assisted contouring, and spatially fractionated radiation therapy. His scientific work has been published in medical journals and presented at international conferences.

He completed radiation oncology fellowships at institutions in Vienna, Schleswig-Holstein, and the US National Cancer Institute in Bethesda. He is a member of the GEC-ESTRO gynecological and breast brachytherapy working groups and serves as an ESTRO FALCON tutor in distance learning and online contouring.

Research and professional interests:

  • Radiation oncology, cervical cancer treatment, and brachytherapy.
  • Personalized radiosensitization and concurrent chemoradiotherapy.
  • AI-assisted contouring, e-learning, and professional training in radiotherapy.
  • Cancer control programs, healthcare project management, and quality assurance.
  • Scientific and technical support for preparedness and response to CBRN threats.

He speaks native Azerbaijani and Russian, fluent English and Turkish, and German at C1 level.

EMBRACE II çoxmərkəzli tədqiqat layihəsində iştirak etmiş, radioterapiyada konturlama üzrə tədris, süni intellektin köməyi ilə konturlama və məkan üzrə fraksiyalaşdırılmış şüa terapiyası sahələrində elmi fəaliyyət göstərmişdir. Elmi işləri tibb jurnallarında dərc olunmuş və beynəlxalq konfranslarda təqdim edilmişdir.

Vyanada, Şlezviq-Holşteyndə və ABŞ-ın Bethesda şəhərində yerləşən Milli Xərçəng İnstitutunda şüa onkologiyası üzrə ixtisasartırma proqramlarını tamamlamışdır. GEC-ESTRO-nun ginekoloji və süd vəzi braxiterapiyası üzrə işçi qruplarının üzvüdür. ESTRO FALCON çərçivəsində distant tədris və onlayn konturlama üzrə təlimçi kimi fəaliyyət göstərir.

Elmi və peşəkar maraq sahələri:

  • Şüa onkologiyası, uşaqlıq boynu xərçənginin müalicəsi və braxiterapiya.
  • Fərdiləşdirilmiş radiosensibilizasiya və eyni vaxtda aparılan kimya-şüa müalicəsi.
