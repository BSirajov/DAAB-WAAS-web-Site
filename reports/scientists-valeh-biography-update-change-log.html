Updated English biography

With the support of the State Program for the Education of Azerbaijani Youth Abroad in 2007-2015, he completed his doctoral studies at the Medical Faculty Mannheim of Heidelberg University with distinction. His main research focuses on creating complex in vitro 3D cell cultures for the study of tumor diseases, and on investigating the effects of drugs on tumor cells in these cultures using various biomarkers.

His work has so far been published in two peer-reviewed articles. His latest article was highly rated by Frontiers in Oncology and republished in a special issue. In addition, software he wrote for the segmentation of large confocal microscopy images is currently at the stage of registration as an invention. As a group leader, he supervises two master's students and one doctoral student.
