Standards and scope

American English: organization, program, specialize, modeling, honor, defense, center, fiber and labor. Official names retain their established spelling. The separate requested house style remains PhD, DSc, MSc, MA, BSc; Dr, Prof, Mr, Mrs and Ms without periods. Academician is spelled out and capitalized when it precedes a name. German Dr.-Ing. is preserved as a distinct qualification.

Month–day–year dates and American quotation punctuation are retained. No dates, counts, degree equivalences, personal names or institutional identities are inferred to make a sentence smoother. Subject areas are not changed merely because someone works across disciplines. Acceptable variants, such as health care/healthcare, are not called spelling errors. Title-style capitalization in affiliation labels is distinguished from capitalization in running prose.

Red marks affected current wording; green marks proposed wording. Insertions or deletions may be visible on only one side. Amber marks wording requiring verification; its correction is described conditionally in the explanation rather than invented.
