Updated English profile

Toghrul Talishinskiy, MD, FACS, FASMBS

Assistant Professor of Surgery
Director of Minimally Invasive and Robotic Surgery, St. Joseph's University Medical Center, Paterson, USA
Director, Minimally Invasive Surgery Fellowship; Chief, Bariatric Surgery

Dr Talishinskiy is a surgeon specializing in minimally invasive, robotic and bariatric surgery. He earned his MD at the American University of the Caribbean School of Medicine, with a full scholarship from the Ministry of Education of Azerbaijan, and completed his general surgery residency at New York University. He then trained in surgical critical care at the University of Michigan and in minimally invasive surgery at Hackensack University Medical Center. He is board certified by the American Board of Surgery, including in surgical critical care, and is a Fellow of the American College of Surgeons and of the American Society for Metabolic and Bariatric Surgery. He also serves as North American Chapter Representative of the Young IFSO (International Federation for the Surgery of Obesity and Metabolic Disorders).

He has authored numerous peer-reviewed publications and a book chapter, and has presented at major international meetings, including IFSO, the American College of Surgeons Clinical Congress, SAGES, Digestive Disease Week and ObesityWeek. He is actively involved in teaching medical students, residents and fellows. He is fluent in English, Russian, Turkish and Azerbaijani.

Research interests: bariatric and metabolic surgery, hernia surgery, core muscle injury, and pediatric surgery.
