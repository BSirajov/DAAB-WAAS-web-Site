Yashar Musayev, Dr.-Ing.

Engineer and technology executive, Germany

Yashar Musayev is an engineer and technology executive specializing in green hydrogen, renewable energy, surface engineering, nanotechnology, and automotive technologies. His international experience combines research and development, industrial production, technology strategy, and collaboration with universities and research institutes.

He studied Mechanical Engineering Technology at Azerbaijan Technical University from 1984 to 1991, graduating with distinction with a Dipl.-Ing. degree. He held a DAAD scholarship at Friedrich-Alexander-Universität Erlangen-Nürnberg (FAU) in 1997–1998 and completed his doctoral research there in 1998–2001, earning the Dr.-Ing. degree. He also participated in Schaeffler’s International Development Program for Top Managers in 2016–2020.

In May 2020, he joined Siemens Energy AG in Erlangen/Munich as Head of Development for New Energy Business. His responsibilities included the development of green hydrogen, electrolysis, and renewable energy technologies, together with technology strategy and team leadership.

From December 2005 to January 2020, he worked at Schaeffler AG in Herzogenaurach. He progressed from Project Leader for establishing the Coating Center to Team Lead and Expert, Director of the Coating Center, and Senior Vice President and Head of the Competency Center for Surface Technology, a position he held from September 2015 to January 2020. His responsibilities covered international research and production activities, coating technologies, supplier development, and partnerships with universities and research institutes.

As a Schaeffler representative, he led the acquisition and integration of Naco Technologies in Riga in December 2015. Alongside his other responsibilities, he served as CEO of SIA Schaeffler Baltic from February 2016 to December 2018, directing research, development, and sales activities in the Baltic States.

His earlier positions included Head of Research and Development at H-O-T in Nuremberg (2001–2005), Research Associate at FAU (2000–2001), Plant Manager at Autostern, a joint-venture partner of Daimler AG in Azerbaijan (1998–2000), and research and teaching roles at Azerbaijan Technical University (1991–1997).

His work since 2005 includes more than 40 inventions and patents and 150 national and international publications and lectures.

Awards and recognition:

  • Customer Supplier Award from a global Japanese automotive manufacturer (2017).
  • Materialica Design + Technology Best-Award for Sensotect® thin-film sensors, Munich (2014, 2016).
  • Customer Innovation Award for engine components from a global Japanese automotive manufacturer (2006).

He is a founding member of Nano in Germany e.V. and a member of the Association of German Engineers (VDI). He has served as Chairman of the Board of BalticNet-Plasmatech e.V. and Baku-Nürnberg e.V.

Research and professional interests:

  • Green hydrogen, electrolysis, renewable energy, and fuel-cell technologies.
  • Surface engineering, coating technologies, nanotechnology, and tribology.
  • Thin-film sensors, automotive technologies, and electric mobility.
  • Research and development management, technology strategy, and international scientific collaboration.

He speaks native Azerbaijani, fluent German, English, and Russian, and conversational Turkish.

  • Nazik təbəqəli sensorlar, avtomobil texnologiyaları və elektrik mobilliyi.
