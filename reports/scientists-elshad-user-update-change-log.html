Updated English biography

Research Professor, Institute for Theoretical Physics II: Soft Matter, Heinrich Heine University Düsseldorf (HHU), Düsseldorf
Professor, Theoretical Physics Division, Joint Institute for High Temperatures, Russian Academy of Sciences, Moscow

Has published more than 200 scientific papers in nearly 40 scientific journals. Serves as a reviewer for nearly 30 journals. Supervises about 10 students. Has taken part in nearly 100 scientific conferences.

Research interests:

  • Morphological changes in proton-conducting ionomers, ionomer conductivity, and development of water-free conductive nanocomposite ionomers.
  • Actuation of elastomer composites, electrostriction effects in dipolar systems, and development of actuating nanocomposites for practical applications.
  • Colloid physics, theory of effective interaction potentials in electrolytes, phase transitions in charged systems, liquid-crystal nucleation, and critical electrolytes in nanopores.
  • Correlation effects in many-body systems, DNA–DNA condensation, and protein–DNA interactions.
  • Nucleation and template-directed phase transitions in dusty plasma systems.
  • Liquid crystals on curved surfaces, defect–defect interactions, and field-dependent morphologies.
  • Novel composite materials for energy-storage applications, and Coulomb correlation effects for induced dipoles.
  • Applications of artificial intelligence and deep learning in materials research.
