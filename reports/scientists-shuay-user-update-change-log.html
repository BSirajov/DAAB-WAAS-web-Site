Updated English biography

He completed his master's degree at Claude Bernard University Lyon 1 in Lyon, France. During his studies, he completed research internships at the Institute of Molecular and Supramolecular Chemistry and Biochemistry and at Wayne State University in Michigan, USA. From 2016 to 2021, he studied at the University of Quebec in Canada, where he earned a PhD in the synthesis of therapeutic nanomaterials.

Since 2022, he has been working as a postdoctoral research scientist at Georgia State University in the USA.

He is the author of 10 scientific papers published in high-impact-factor international journals. His h-index, according to the Scopus database, is 4.

His research focuses on the bioactive chemical synthesis of carbohydrate molecules. In particular, he has published papers on the efficient synthesis of di- and trisaccharides used in cancer diagnostics in Carbohydrate Research and the International Journal of Molecular Sciences. He has developed a new and efficient synthesis of migalastat, a drug used to treat the rare Fabry disease, and plans to file a patent application for this methodology.
